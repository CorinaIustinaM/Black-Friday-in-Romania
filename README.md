# Black-Friday-in-Romania: Planned Purchases or Impulse Shopping

## Project Overview
This project analyzes MKOR survey indicators (2023-2026) and NETOPIA card payment data, investigating trends over time and differences across shopper segments (buyers, undecided and non-buyers), shopping channels, product categories and payment behaviour.

## Research Question
Do Romanian shoppers really plan their Black Friday purchases, or do they shop impulsively?

## Research Hypothesis
Most Romanian Black Friday shoppers plan their purchases weeks in advance, but their final purchase decisions are influenced by the perceived authenticity and attractiveness of discounts, as well as product availability.

## Data Source
- MKOR report "Black Friday Trends 2026": https://mkor.ro/studii/black-friday-2026-romania/
- Dataset on Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/romaniandatatribe/planned-or-impulsive-black-friday-in-romania

## Tool Used
- Microsoft Power BI

## Conclusions
1. Planning is prevalent
- Most shoppers prepare for Black Friday in advance, setting budgets and making shopping lists approximately four weeks before the event.

2. Discounts still matter
- The attractiveness and perceived authenticity of discounts remain important considerations, both for buyers and undecided shoppers.

3. Intentions and actual behaviour are not the same
- Survey data show a growing share of respondents intending to buy, while transaction data reveal that card payments are spread across the period surrounding Black Friday, rather than concentrated exclusively on the event day.

Overall, the findings suggest that Black Friday shopping in Romania is largely planned, but purchase decisions remain sensitive to price, product availability and the shopping experience.
